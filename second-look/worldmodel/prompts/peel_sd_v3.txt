Raw standard-definition laparoscope footage during gallbladder surgery: soft, slightly muddy image, muted colours, faint noise in the shadows. Circular black endoscope mask cropped top and bottom. Nearby tissue bright, background falling into darkness. Magenta and purple-pink colour cast. Handheld camera close to the tissue, drifting slightly, everything glistening with moisture.

The distended gallbladder fills the upper left: a mauve-violet wall with fine red vessels under a glossy surface. The liver is smooth, glossy, deep maroon, almost black in shadow. Dull yellow-orange fat along the lower edge. The dissected tissue around the gallbladder neck is raw and matte, unevenly stained dark reddish-brown soaked into the surface, with irregular brownish burnt edges.

Only two instruments are in view. A fenestrated grasper enters from the left edge, holds the gallbladder neck, pulls it gently left and down and stays almost perfectly still. A long laparoscopic electrocautery hook enters from the upper right: a slim straight grey insulated shaft, several centimetres long, ending in a tiny bent metal tip. No hands are visible; only the shafts of the two laparoscopic instruments.

At the start the area between the gallbladder neck and the liver is already cleared: a shadowed gap in the tissue reveals dark liver surface behind it, and exactly two separate pale pinkish-white bands run into the gallbladder neck, a wider one below and a thinner one above. The lower gallbladder still lies flat against the liver.

Over about six seconds the grasper lifts the gallbladder neck slightly up and away from the liver while the hook tip stays on the tissue, moving in short strokes along the junction between gallbladder and liver and peeling the lower gallbladder free; thin tissue tents under the tip and springs back. The hook then withdraws straight back along its shaft and leaves the frame, and the view holds for the last two seconds. At the end a strip of smooth, raw, whitish-pink tissue with brownish burnt edges is visible on the liver side beneath the lifted lower gallbladder, while the upper gallbladder stays attached. The two bands and the shadowed gap remain unchanged.
