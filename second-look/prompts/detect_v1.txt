This is one frame from a laparoscopic cholecystectomy (keyhole gallbladder removal), taken during dissection of the
hepatocystic triangle. Detect the anatomy and instruments below and return a bounding box for each one you can see.

Classes:
- gallbladder: the pear-shaped organ being removed, usually held up by a grasper.
- liver: the large dark red-brown organ behind the gallbladder.
- cystic_duct: tubular biliary structure leaving the gallbladder neck (will be clipped).
- cystic_artery: thinner tubular vessel to the gallbladder, usually above or behind the duct (will be clipped).
- cystic_plate: connective tissue exposed on the liver bed where the gallbladder has been lifted off.
- hepatocystic_window: a dissected gap in the hepatocystic triangle you can see through.
- grasper, hook, clipper, scissors, irrigator: instruments; box the instrument tip area in view.

Only box what is actually visible. Leave a class out rather than guess. A tubular structure still covered in fat is not
yet a cystic_duct or cystic_artery unless its shape is clearly visible.

Return JSON only:
{"boxes": [{"label": "<class>", "box_2d": [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax], "confidence": <0.0-1.0>}]}
Coordinates are integers from 0 to 1000, relative to the image height (y) and width (x).
